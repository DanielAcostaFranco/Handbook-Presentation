# Presentation Notes

## Audience

BYUI FTC engineering team members and engineering leadership.

## Purpose

Introduce the proposed BYUI FTC Engineering Handbook and explain the practical changes the team will adopt when planning, building, reviewing, testing, deploying, and maintaining projects.

## Suggested timing

- Opening and reason for change: 2 minutes.
- Roles and approval: 3 minutes.
- Repository, branch, and security standards: 4 minutes.
- Hosting and Apps Script: 3 minutes.
- Benefits and next steps: 2 minutes.

## Speaker reminders

- The handbook standardizes the process, not every technology.
- The Owner is always BYUI FTC.
- Employees identify problems and propose solutions.
- The Engineering Lead or Engineering Student Lead makes the approval decision in consultation with other engineering leads and a Full-Time Employee.
- Local hooks provide early feedback, but GitHub branch protection is the authoritative enforcement layer.
- All application code and documentation must remain in the official GitHub repository regardless of where the application is hosted.
- Staging is optional, but when used it comes before Production.
- Leave visual examples as placeholders until the team decides which projects and screenshots to show.
